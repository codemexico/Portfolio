Applied engineering, scientific computing, and instrumentation projects: physics-based modeling, numerical methods, machine learning, and computational tools developed for diverse applications.

RiskGate: Portfolio and Options Risk System

Co-developed a Python-based decision-support system integrating equity backtesting, Black–Scholes option valuation with GARCH volatility, statistical arbitrage, position sizing, and automated paper trading through Alpaca.

Approach

The system produces a 0–100 approval score combining market regime, technical signals, option quality, and process discipline, with hard stops and risk-budget-based sizing. Strategies were evaluated across nine equities using 2022–2026 market data, 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations, and paper-trading experiments, while documenting concentration, execution, and model risks.

3D Reconstruction by Fringe Projection Profilometry

Design and implementation of an optical profilometry method that reconstructs the three-dimensional shape of real objects from projected fringe patterns. The system combines an experimental projection setup with a complete numerical processing chain.

Approach

Four-step phase-shifting was used to extract the wrapped phase, followed by phase unwrapping, geometric calibration, and a height model to convert phase into physical depth. Robust filtering and object masking were applied to clean the measurements, and the method was validated against a reference sphere to quantify reconstruction accuracy.

Rotary Cutter Dynamic Model

Complete dynamic modeling of a rotary cutter system using the Euler–Lagrange formalism with dissipative Rayleigh terms. The system is modeled as a set of coupled nonlinear differential equations describing the rotational motion of the disk and blades under realistic operating conditions. The project was sold to John Deere´s R&D Team.

Approach

Numerical integration via fourth-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) to simulate the system's response, enabling analysis of torque requirements, blade dynamics, and energy dissipation. The model bridges theoretical mechanics with an applied industrial context.

Heat Diffusion in Steel Annealing

Numerical modeling of heat diffusion in steel coils during the annealing process used in industrial bell furnaces. A physically motivated diffusion model was implemented to capture the transient thermal behavior of the material under controlled furnace conditions.

Approach

The project emphasizes the relationship between thermal diffusivity, geometry, and process time, providing insight into how annealing duration can be optimized without compromising material quality. Results are supported by visual animations illustrating the evolution of the temperature field.

Optical Telescope Construction

Complete design, construction, calibration, and characterization of a functional optical telescope. The project includes theoretical calculations of magnification, focal lengths, and resolution, as well as ray-tracing simulations based on the physical dimensions of the prototype.

Approach

Experimental calibration techniques were applied to quantify measurement uncertainty and image quality. The project documents both the theoretical framework and experimental methodology, supported by original photographs of the telescope and observed astronomical objects.

Wireless Luminance Meter for Displays

Design and construction of a handheld wireless instrument for measuring display luminance, combining a VEML7700 digital sensor with an LDR reference on an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi. The device includes an LCD, LED range indicators, and a 3D-printed ergonomic housing, and was validated through an experimental characterization of linearity, hysteresis, repeatability, and uncertainty.

My Role

I was mainly responsible for the software and connectivity: the microcontroller code for acquisition, processing, and state indication; the wireless link that logs measurements to the ThingSpeak IoT platform; and the visualization interfaces, including a MATLAB GUI and a custom live web dashboard.

Exoplanet Detection Using Neural Networks

Implementation of an automated pipeline for the classification of exoplanet transit signals from TESS light-curve data. Physical preprocessing techniques, including detrending and period detection, are combined with a one-dimensional convolutional neural network (CNN).

Approach

The model outputs probabilistic classifications distinguishing confirmed exoplanets from false positives. The project demonstrates the integration of astrophysical theory with modern machine learning, visual outputs, and a standalone executable application.

Data-Driven Stochastic Optimization of Inventory and Pricing Policies

An integrated framework for the stochastic optimization of inventory management and pricing strategies in a real-world economic system. The methodology combines predictive modeling, Monte Carlo simulation, and hybrid optimization techniques to address uncertainty and variability in demand.

Approach

A neural network model forecasts demand based on historical data, capturing nonlinear relationships and temporal patterns. These predictions are embedded into a stochastic simulation of daily operations, evaluating system performance under fluctuating conditions. Optimization is performed using a genetic algorithm to explore the decision space, followed by simulated annealing to refine solutions and improve convergence. The framework provides a scalable and generalizable approach to multi-objective optimization, balancing profitability and operational stability.

Stochastic Optimization of Packing Fraction Using Simulated Annealing

Development of a simulated-compression algorithm for maximizing the packing fraction of hard disks confined within a two-dimensional box. The algorithm determines dense, non-overlapping configurations by stochastically optimizing both particle positions and the effective size of the containing domain.

Approach

The algorithm proposes random displacements of particles, followed by an evaluation of an objective function that penalizes overlaps and inefficient spatial arrangements. Configuration updates are accepted or rejected according to a Metropolis-type criterion. A compression mechanism gradually reduces the confining box size, coupling configurational optimization with geometric compression. As the temperature decreases, the algorithm transitions from exploratory stochastic motion to deterministic refinement, converging toward configurations with high packing fraction.
