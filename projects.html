RiskGate: Portfolio and Options Risk System

Quantitative finance | Python, GARCH, Monte Carlo, Alpaca API

Co-developed a Python-based decision-support system integrating equity backtesting, Black–Scholes option valuation with GARCH volatility, statistical arbitrage, position sizing, and automated paper trading through Alpaca.

Approach. The system produces a 0–100 approval score combining market regime, technical signals, option quality, and process discipline, with hard stops and risk-budget-based sizing. Strategies were evaluated across nine equities using 2022–2026 market data, 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations, and paper-trading experiments, while documenting concentration, execution, and model risks.

3D Reconstruction by Fringe Projection Profilometry

Experimental optics | Python, phase-shifting interferometry, image processing, uncertainty analysis

Design and implementation of a full optical profilometry pipeline that reconstructs the three-dimensional shape of real objects from projected fringe patterns. The system combines an experimental projection setup with a complete numerical processing chain.

Approach. Four-step phase-shifting was used to extract the wrapped phase, followed by phase unwrapping, geometric calibration, and a height model to convert phase into physical depth. Robust filtering and object masking were applied to clean the measurements, and the method was validated against a reference sphere to quantify reconstruction accuracy.

Rotary Cutter Dynamic Model

John Deere collaboration | Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, Tkinter

Complete dynamic modeling of a rotary cutter system using the Euler–Lagrange formalism with dissipative Rayleigh terms. The system is modeled as a set of coupled nonlinear differential equations describing the rotational motion of the disk and blades under realistic operating conditions.

Approach. Numerical integration via fourth-order Runge–Kutta to simulate the system's response, enabling analysis of torque requirements, blade dynamics, and energy dissipation.

Heat Diffusion in Steel Annealing

Ternium collaboration | MATLAB, heat equation

Numerical modeling of heat diffusion in steel coils during the annealing process used in industrial bell furnaces. A physically motivated diffusion model was implemented to capture the transient thermal behavior of the material under controlled furnace conditions.

Approach. The project emphasizes the relationship between thermal diffusivity, geometry, and process time, providing insight into how annealing duration can be optimized without compromising material quality.

Optical Telescope Construction

Experimental optics | Optical design, ray tracing, Python, uncertainty analysis

Complete design, construction, calibration, and characterization of a functional optical telescope. The project includes theoretical calculations of magnification, focal lengths, and resolution, as well as ray-tracing simulations based on the physical dimensions of the prototype.

Approach. Experimental calibration techniques were applied to quantify measurement uncertainty and image quality, documenting both the theoretical framework and the experimental methodology.

Wireless Luminance Meter for Displays

Electronic instrumentation, team project | Arduino/C++, MATLAB, ThingSpeak, HTML/JS

Design and construction of a handheld wireless instrument for measuring display luminance, combining a VEML7700 digital sensor with an LDR reference on an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi. The device includes an LCD, LED range indicators, and a 3D-printed ergonomic housing, and was validated through an experimental characterization of linearity, hysteresis, repeatability, and uncertainty.

My role. I was mainly responsible for the software and connectivity: the microcontroller code for acquisition, processing, and state indication; the wireless link that logs measurements to the ThingSpeak IoT platform; and the visualization interfaces, including a MATLAB GUI and a custom live web dashboard.

Stochastic Optimization of Inventory and Pricing Policies

Machine learning and metaheuristics | Python, neural networks, Monte Carlo, genetic algorithms

An integrated framework for the stochastic optimization of inventory management and pricing strategies. The methodology combines predictive modeling, Monte Carlo simulation, and hybrid optimization techniques to address uncertainty and variability in demand.

Approach. A neural network model forecasts demand based on historical data, capturing nonlinear relationships and temporal patterns. These predictions are embedded into a stochastic simulation of daily operations, evaluating system performance under fluctuating conditions. Optimization is performed using a genetic algorithm to explore the decision space, followed by simulated annealing to refine solutions and improve convergence.

Packing Fraction Optimization by Simulated Annealing

Geometric optimization | Python, NumPy, simulated annealing, Metropolis algorithm

Development of a simulated-compression algorithm for maximizing the packing fraction of hard disks confined within a two-dimensional box. The algorithm determines dense, non-overlapping configurations by stochastically optimizing both particle positions and the effective size of the containing domain.

Approach. The algorithm proposes random displacements of particles, followed by an evaluation of an objective function that penalizes overlaps and inefficient spatial arrangements. Configuration updates are accepted or rejected according to a Metropolis-type criterion, while a compression mechanism gradually reduces the confining box size. As the temperature decreases, the algorithm transitions from exploratory stochastic motion to deterministic refinement.
