About Me

I am a Physics Engineering student with experience developing computational models, simulation workflows, and data-driven decision-support tools for industrial and financial systems. My background includes finite-element analysis and Python automation for digital twins, physics-based modeling of mechanical and thermal systems, and statistical analysis and dashboarding for large datasets.

My current research spans nonlinear dynamical systems, Koopman and transfer-operator methods, data-driven modeling and control, and theoretical work in variational principles and general relativity, implemented in Python, Julia, and MATLAB. I am particularly interested in the intersection of physics, optimization, and machine learning, with applications ranging from advanced simulation and nanoelectronics to quantitative finance.
