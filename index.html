About Me

I am a Physics Engineering student with experience developing computational and ML models, simulation workflows, and data-driven tools for industrial and financial systems. My background includes FEM analysis, automation for digital twins, research on computational methods, statistical analysis for large datasets, and financial research for portfolio management.

My current research spans nonlinear dynamical systems, Koopman and transfer-operator methods, data-driven modeling and control, and theoretical work in contact Hamiltonians and ML, implemented in Python, Julia, and MATLAB. I am particularly interested in the intersection of physics, optimization, and machine learning, with applications ranging from advanced simulation to quantitative finance.
